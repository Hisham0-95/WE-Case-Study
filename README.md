## Project Overview

A high-stakes case study completed for the Strategic Research Specialist role at WE Telecom. The assessment involved analyzing a large-scale telecom dataset across 9 tables — including 4 fact tables with millions of rows — and delivering actionable insights and strategic recommendations through a professional Power BI dashboard within a strict 4-day deadline.

## Core Challenges & How I Overcame Them

### 1. Large-Scale Data Handling
**Challenge:** First exposure to true enterprise-scale data — 9 tables including 4 fact tables with millions of rows, common in telecom environments.

**Achievement:** Successfully managed the full dataset, navigated performance hurdles, and built confidence in handling enterprise-level data volumes efficiently.

### 2. Data Contextualization Without Metadata
**Challenge:** The dataset lacked metadata, making column interpretation complex and hindering initial cleaning efforts.

**Achievement:** Proactively used industry research, Google, and AI tools to rapidly build contextual understanding of standard telecom data structures — enabling effective cleaning and relevant insight generation.

### 3. Mastering Advanced DAX Measures
**Challenge:** The assessment demanded highly specific answers requiring complex DAX measures — a skill area less developed than my SQL proficiency at the time.

**Achievement:** Identified the gap immediately, dedicated focused effort to rapidly upskill in advanced DAX, and successfully modeled the data to deliver precise, measurable answers to the business questions.

### 4. High-Pressure, Time-Constrained Delivery
**Challenge:** Strict 4-day deadline to complete analysis and deliver actionable results.

**Achievement:** Through disciplined time management, successfully completed 5 out of 10 complex questions — each with a data-driven insight and a strategic recommendation aligned with WE Telecom's business objectives.

## Conclusion

This project was a definitive growth milestone. I delivered quality analysis under pressure, identified a critical skill gap in DAX, and transformed it into a focused development area — significantly improving my proficiency within a short timeframe.

This experience solidified my ability to work with enterprise-scale data, adapt quickly under pressure, and deliver business value through analytics.
